Run events

Besides fighting and levelling up, things happen in every run: events that ask you for something, coin thieves, Team Rocket, weather and map objects. In a group everyone sees them and the reward goes to everyone.

Events (every 4-5 minutes)

The first one comes between minutes 4 and 5, then another one 4-5 minutes after the previous one ends. The same one never happens twice in a row.

Type horde

For 30 s every enemy is of one type. If your moves are super effective against it, it's your moment!

Golden hour

30 s in which enemies turn golden: double experience and sometimes coins.

Lost Pokémon

Touch it so it follows you and take it to the green mark within 2 minutes, without enemies hitting it. Reward: heals half your HP, 60 coins and a ticket, and 1 in 5 times it joins your collection.

Hungry Munchlax

Shake the bushes (walk over them) and bring it 3 berries like the one it wants. Careful, two bushes hide a swarm of Combee! Reward: an item for each player and an Exp. Candy.

Coin thieves

Up to 2 per run (the first between minutes 2 and 4). A Meowth loaded with coins (100) or, 1 in 4 times, a Gholdengo with a ×10 ticket. They don't attack, but they zigzag away: you have 15 s to catch them.

Team Rocket and their casino

In 1 of every 3 runs that reach the Judgment (solo only), Team Rocket snatches your Pokémon to the Rocket Casino. The run clock stops. You can leave right away or play with your Pokécoins: Slots (the jackpot gives the Team Rocket trail, which isn't sold), Hidden Voltorb, the Rocket Race and Catch Pikachu!, a skill game in three plans. In the long run, the house wins.

Map objects

Power altar

Touch it for a 25 s power: damage ×2, +45 % speed, magnet ×4 or −40 % cooldown.

Spring

Standing on it heals 14 % of your HP per second until it dries up; it refills while you're nearby.

Locked chest

Stay next to it for 3.5 s: coins, tickets, orbs, items and the experience for a whole level.

Traps

Poison, sticky or explosive: it goes off when stepped on, enemies too! Lure them in.

Also: Pokémon tides about every minute, and a wild shiny now and then (1 in 4,096): defeat it and it's yours, shiny.
