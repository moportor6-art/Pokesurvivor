Powers: Mega Evolution, Dynamax, Z-Moves and Terastal

If you beat Eternatus at minute 20, you pick a power for the rest of the run. Use it with F (X on a gamepad; on mobile, its round button, which shows up when it's ready). It lasts a few seconds and recharges on its own over time.

How to get one

  • When you beat Eternatus you get 3 or 4 cards depending on your Pokémon: Mega Evolution (or Fusion / Form Change) if it has one, Gigantamax if it has that form (otherwise Dynamax), the Z-Power Ring and Terastal.
  • It's one per run: it can't be changed or kept for the next run. If you fall against Eternatus, there's no power.
  • In co-op, if you win, everyone picks their own.
  • The power bar sits under your HP: it fills up on its own while recharging and glows when it's ready.

Mega Evolution

  • Its Mega's types, stats and ability for 40 s (HP doesn't change).
  • The new stats keep your proportions: your run level, nature and Upgrades still count.
  • Those with two (Charizard X or Y, Mewtwo X or Y…) pick one when they take the power.
  • Kyogre and Groudon return to their Primal form (with rain or sun); Mega Charizard Y brings sun.
  • 85 Pokémon have a Mega.

Fusions and power forms

  • Same card, same rules as the Mega.
  • Necrozma (Dusk Mane, Dawn Wings or Ultra Necrozma), Kyurem (White or Black), Calyrex (Ice or Shadow Rider), Zacian, Zamazenta, Complete Zygarde and Ash-Greninja.
  • Solgaleo, Lunala, Reshiram, Zekrom, Glastrier and Spectrier can fuse from their half too.

Dynamax and Gigantamax

  • It turns giant, doubles its HP and deals 40 % more damage; every hit splashes nearby enemies.
  • It breaks what it steps on (trees, rocks, walls, chests) and crushes regular Pokémon.
  • The 32 with a Gigantamax form release a G-Max wave every 3 s with an effect: heal, burn, stun, slow, poison, chain, shield…
  • It doesn't heal during the Judgment of Arceus.

Z-Power Ring

  • Pick a type (or your Pokémon's exclusive Z: Pikachu, Eevee, Snorlax, Mew, Mimikyu…).
  • For 15 s, a big Z rolls out every 0.8 s and goes through everything.
  • At the end, the Z-Move: a hit on every enemy on screen.

Terastal

  • Pick one of the 18 types (or Stellar): your Pokémon becomes that type only.
  • Its moves of that type hit ×1.5, or ×1.8 if it was already one of its types.
  • Stellar: it keeps its types and all its moves hit ×1.3.

Pokémon forms

Regional

Alolan Vulpix, Galarian Meowth, Hisuian Growlithe… They're separate Pokémon: they come from the gacha and have their own types and stats. There are 57.

Type and stat changes

Deoxys, Rotom, Arceus, Shaymin, Hoopa, Oricorio… After the 3rd rift (minute 12) you get to pick its form once, for the rest of the run.

Battle forms

Aegislash, Wishiwashi, Darmanitan, Mimikyu, Eiscue, Castform, Morpeko, Palafin… They change on their own depending on what happens.

Cosmetic

Unown letters, Vivillon patterns, Alcremie, female forms… Free in the Dressing Room: they only change the look.

Tip: power numbers are launch values and may be tuned once there's data from real runs.
