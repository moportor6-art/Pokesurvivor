PokéSurvivor moves and their evolutions

There are 176 moves across all 18 types. Your Pokémon attacks on its own with the active one, and when you level up you pick cards to learn new moves or upgrade them up to level 5. 149 of them can evolve: max them out, get the upgrade card they ask for (Power, Vigor, Reflexes…) and a golden evolution card will appear.

Which stat does each one use? Just like in the Pokémon games: physical moves hit with your Pokémon's Attack and special moves with its Special Attack. With its strong side it hits at 100 %; with the other one, in proportion (a Pokémon with 120 Attack and 60 Special Attack hits half as hard with special moves). Each move's card tells you how well it works with your Pokémon. Enemies defend against physical moves with their Defense and against special ones with their Special Defense.
