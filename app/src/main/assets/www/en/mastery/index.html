Mastery: play with your favourite Pokémon

Just like in the Pokémon games, playing a lot with a Pokémon earns you its mastery. Every Pokémon has its own, and it takes effort.

How you earn points

  • 1 point for every 6 Pokémon defeated and 25 for every minute survived with it as your partner, solo or in a group.
  • A full run gives about 1,600 points (a bit more than half from defeated Pokémon). At most 40 minutes of a run count.
  • They go to the Pokémon you chose, even if it evolves during the run.
  • The weekly challenge (where you don't choose the Pokémon) and tampered runs don't count. With an account, the server confirms the points at the end.

The rewards

  • 10,000Mastery photoA special face of it that is no longer sold in the Kecleon Shop.about 6 full runs
  • 25,000Master title“… Master” for your profile.about 16 full runs
  • 50,000Mastery frameThe golden frame with gems of its type and its face on the medallion.about 32 full runs
  • 100,000Master auraA golden glow and sparkles when you play with it; your group sees it too.about 63 full runs

Past 100,000 points keep adding up with no cap: you can always see how much mastery you have with each Pokémon. You'll find yours on your partner card and in Mastery, in the main menu: all your Pokémon from most to least mastery, with a search box, and each one's page with its numbers (runs, time played, defeated, Arceus reached, best time, bosses, rifts and Eternatus). From there, or from Missions → Mastery, you put on the photo, title and frame.
